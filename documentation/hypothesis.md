## Hypotheses

Vaccine beliefs are associated with vaccination uptake.
Demographics are associated with vaccination uptake.
Doctor recommendations are associated with higher uptake.
Chronic illness is associated with higher uptake.
H1N1 and seasonal vaccination behaviours are related.

Are people with chronic illnesses more likely to get vaccinated?

Are vaccine beliefs and risk perception associated with uptake?
Are seasonal-vaccine recipients more likely to receive the H1N1 vaccine?
